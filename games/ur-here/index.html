ABOUT

UR HERE · PROTOTYPE 1

A living infographic about where we are in time. Ride the train from Earth's formation to the red giant sun. Get off at each station and find Sunni, the guide whose head is a sundial. His rooster Gallus, nature's clock, crows from wherever Sunni is. Listen for him.

The track is the timeline. The past is drawn in ink. The future, from "UR HERE" on, is drawn in pencil, because it's a prediction rather than a record.

Switch scales with the tabs: all of Earth's story, the last million years, or one human life. Colour is highlighter on paper: green is life, blue is water, yellow is the sun, orange is fire. The future has no colour yet.
