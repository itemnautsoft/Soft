Key breath is how hard a key blows when you aren't using the mic or the Air strip. Slide a finger sideways on a key to bend the pitch, like lipping a note. With mic breath on, keys only sound while you blow: aim at the bottom of the phone, near the mic. Headphones help.

Computer: keys A–; and Q–T play, Space blows.

TONPET

A wind instrument somewhere between a trumpet and a bassoon. Its tone comes from a modelled air column and lip-reed, driven by your breath. Turn off silent mode and turn the sound up.
