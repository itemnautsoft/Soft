Recording and sheet

Key breath is how hard a key blows when you aren't using the mic or the Air strip. Slide a finger sideways on a key to bend the pitch. With Mic on, keys only sound while you blow at the bottom of the phone. Headphones help. Recording snaps your notes to the note grid.

Computer: keys A–; and Q–T play, Space blows. On the sheet: Delete removes, arrows move, Ctrl+Z undoes.

TONPET

A wind instrument somewhere between a trumpet and a bassoon. Its tone comes from a modelled air column and lip-reed, driven by your breath. Turn off silent mode and turn the sound up.
